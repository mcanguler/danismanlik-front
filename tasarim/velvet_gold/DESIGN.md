## Brand & Style
This design system embodies high-end feminine empowerment, transformative relationship and family counseling, and boutique digital commerce. The aesthetic blends classical editorial luxury with warm, empathetic modernism. It evokes a safe, opulent sanctuary where vulnerability is met with grace, authority, and emotional resonance.

The target audience comprises discerning women, modern couples, and families seeking refined therapeutic guidance, transformational courses, masterclasses, and curated lifestyle tools. 

The visual strategy merges **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous, breathable negative space allowing editorial serif typography to breathe.
- Warm champagne, ivory, and cashmere canvas surfaces layered with velvety rose-tinted modules.
- Intimate, intentional accents of deep wine burgundy for grounded authority and call-to-action prominence.
- Gilded metallic accents (warm gold and rose gold) acting as polished jewelry across badges, dividers, and micro-interactions.

## Colors
The color palette balances deep, grounded emotional gravitas with luminous, comforting warmth.

- **Primary (`#5C1D24` / Deep Wine Burgundy):** Serves as the authoritative backbone. Used for high-impact conversion triggers, dominant headings, primary button fills, and structural framing. Its sibling `#78242E` is reserved for gentle interactive states and gradient transitions.
- **Secondary (`#E8A5A5` / Dusty Rose):** Provides soft, feminine gentleness. Utilized in high-touch interactive states, subtle highlight tints, and secondary button motifs. Supported by `#F8E7E7` and `#F2C4CE` for tinted background surfaces, alert ribbons, and notification pills.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059` & `#D4AF37` / Warm & Polished Gold):** Represents elegance, elevation, and value. Applied to discount badges, ratings, luxury product markers, card borders, icons, and subtle ornamental rules.
- **Neutral (`#2B2B2B` / Rich Charcoal):** Replaces harsh standard blacks to preserve warmth and readability across editorial blocks.
- **Surfaces (`#FAF8F5` & `#FFFDFB`):** Establish the ambient base tone—creamy, radiant, and gentle on the eyes, eschewing sterile cool whites.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy brings together **Playfair Display** for editorial storytelling and romantic sophistication with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for clear, open, and welcoming user interfaces.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with Playfair Display. The generous x-height and high stroke contrast invoke high-end publication layouts. Italicized accents within subheadings are encouraged to punctuate key emotional keywords (e.g., *healing*, *intimacy*, *wholeness*).
- **Body & Functional Text:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with generous line heights to provide effortless readability and an open, uncluttered cadence.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase transformations combined with wide tracking (`0.04em` to `0.1em`) are strictly reserved for badges, breadcrumbs, category eyebrows, and small navigation links to create a refined editorial tone.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a classical 12-column grid system centered within a maximum width of `1320px` to foster a spacious, boutique presentation.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` canvas margins. Editorial sections alternate between compact centered columns (8 cols) for counseling reflections and expansive 12-col asymmetric arrangements for luxury products and service showcases.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` side margins. Multi-column cards compress into clean 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Nested elements collapse vertically into stacked sequences with tactile touch targets (minimum 48px height).

Spacing between discrete editorial sections utilizes generous vertical breathing room (`space-xl` scaled up to `6rem` for major thematic shifts), emphasizing unhurried pacing and clarity.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through warm, radiant luminosity rather than cold structural drop shadows.

- **Ambient Glowing Shadows:** Elevated components use soft, multi-layered shadows infused with warm champagne and dusty rose tints. The default floating card shadow is defined as `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(92, 29, 36, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.
- **Delicate Border Accents:** Flat surfaces retain definition through whisper-thin borders (`1px solid #EFE6DE` or `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`). Gold hairline borders are applied selectively to premier offerings and elevated carts.
- **Surface Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#FAF8F5`. Cards, counseling modules, and modal containers sit on elevated pure surfaces of `#FFFDFB` or soft blush tints (`#F8E7E7`), establishing immediate spatial differentiation without visual clutter.

## Shapes
The shape language features custom luxury curves that soften edges without sacrificing architectural elegance:

- Standard elements (cards, counseling feature boxes, input containers) adopt a refined 16px (`1rem` / standard rounded-lg token) corner radius.
- Small components such as luxury pill badges, tag chips, and micro-buttons utilize full-pill radii (`9999px`) to contrast with geometric editorial framing.
- Image containers alternate between softly rounded corners (`16px`) and classical arched tops (`border-top-left-radius: 120px; border-top-right-radius: 120px;`) for founder portraits, session previews, and featured counseling services.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled deep burgundy (`#5C1D24`) with creamy crisp text (`#FFFDFB`), soft pill or 14px rounded curvature, and a subtle glowing shadow. On hover, transitions seamlessly to `#78242E` with an elevation increase.
- **Secondary Button:** Soft blush surface (`#F8E7E7`) with burgundy text (`#5C1D24`) and a delicate border (`#E8A5A5`). Hover shifts background to `#F2C4CE`.
- **Tertiary / Editorial Link:** Underlined rich text or uppercase tracked label accompanied by a delicate trailing arrow icon, highlighted with warm gold (`#C5A059`) upon interaction.

### Cards & Luxury Modules
- Built on `#FFFDFB` or translucent backdrop-blur surfaces with 16px corner radius and hairline gold/neutral borders (`#EFE6DE`).
- Feature generous internal padding (`space-lg`), high-contrast Playfair typography, and floating luxury badges positioned in the top-right corner.

### Badges & Discount Pills
- **Discount & Special Offer Badges:** Pill-shaped, rendered in warm gold (`#C5A059`) with dark burgundy or white text, or pale blush backgrounds with gold foil borders.
- **Category & Status Chips:** Set in uppercase, tracking `0.08em`, small label font (`11px`/`12px`), utilizing subtle blush or cream fills with neutral charcoal text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Clean inputs with `#FAF8F5` background, 1px border in `#EFE6DE`, and 12px border radius.
- Focused state replaces the default ring with a soft, warm burgundy hairline glow (`rgba(92, 29, 36, 0.15)`) and border color `#5C1D24`. Placeholder text is set in muted warm gray.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular radios and rounded checkboxes with 1.5px borders in `#C5A059`.
- Checked state fills with deep burgundy (`#5C1D24`) revealing an ivory checkmark or central warm gold dot.

### Counseling & Booking Slates
- Special component for session reservations: dual-pane schedule layout featuring calendar dates encased in circular hover rings and pricing summary anchored by gold divider accents.
